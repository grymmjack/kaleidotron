Perfect DOS VGA 437 — a pixel-perfect recreation of the IBM PC / DOS VGA
text font (code page 437), by Zeh Fernando. Distributed as freeware,
free to use in any project. https://www.zone38.net / https://zeh.com.br

This bundle is the "Nerd Font" patched build (PerfectDOSVGA437NerdFontMono),
which adds the Nerd Fonts icon glyph set (icons from Font Awesome, Devicons,
Octicons, Powerline, Material Design, Weather, etc.). The Nerd Fonts patcher
and its icon aggregation are released under the MIT License.
Nerd Fonts: https://www.nerdfonts.com  (© Ryan L McIntyre, MIT)

We use it here purely as a bitmap-accurate rasterization source for the
Unicode text-art ("Ramp") converter — its CP437 block/shade/box glyphs and
Nerd Font icons render crisply on their native pixel grid.
